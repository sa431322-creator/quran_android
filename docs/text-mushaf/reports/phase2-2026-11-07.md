# Phase 2 proof — ayah cards — 2026-11-07

Branch `tasnim/text-mushaf`. Design approved on the
[ayah cards canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/JCgRivTw7Adkd41n3MDtGP) (A · gilt look). Decisions:
the card's Persian text is «جامع البیان – ترجمۀ تفسیری» only (bundled, offline); the reader's middle
tab opens the cards; tablets 840 dp and wider show two columns; cards carry play and bookmark, the
rest of the ayah actions on long-press.

## What a card is

- The ayah in the mushaf's own glyphs: each word drawn with the V2 font of the page it is on (an
  ayah over a page turn keeps each word with its page), wrapped right to left to the card. The
  glyphs are the page's, unchanged; only the line breaks differ. The end marker is the page's gilt
  marker. In tajweed mode the cards use the tajweed (V4) fonts.
- Its number in the gilt star, ▶ play from this ayah, bookmark.
- Under it the Persian text of «جامع البیان», with a button to open or close just this card; one
  switch at the top shows or hides it on every card (kept across launches).
- Each sura starts with the page's gilt header and the basmala.
- The text size follows the app's text-size setting; night mode follows the reader's.

## In the reader

- The «ترجمه و تفسیر فارسی» tab shows the cards at the selected ayah or the page's first ayah;
  «مصحف» and «تجويد» go back to the pages at the card on top (the pages follow the cards as they
  scroll, so the title, page slider and last page agree).
- Long-press a card: the reader's ayah toolbar over the card (bookmark, share, translation, tafsir
  panel, play). A tap shows or hides the bars, as on a page.
- Recitation: the recited card is tinted and brought on screen.
- Rotation keeps the cards (phone and tablet).
