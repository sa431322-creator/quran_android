## 4. Reader features (checked on the emulator)

Text mode is the default page type; images are an option in Settings and the choice is kept.
Long-press selection and the ayah toolbar, the Persian tafsir panel, bookmarks, last page,
recitation highlight, sura/juz/page jumps, two-page tablet view and night mode work on the text
pages through the reader's own `AyahTrackerPresenter`. Ayah bookmarks and recent pages are stored
by ayah, so nothing had to move.

## 5. Step 9: no trial code, one reader

- Removed: `TextMushafTrialActivity`, its debug menu entry, `MADINAH_V2_TRIAL`, the trial
  assets (including the KSU reference images in debug assets).
- Tajweed is now the reader's own text pages drawn with the V4 tajweed fonts: the «تجويد» tab
  switches the pages in place (both mushafs hold the same ayahs on every page), and the home
  «مصحف تجوید» button opens the reader at the last page in tajweed. The separate
  `TajweedReaderActivity`, its DI graph, layout and sharing helper are gone (about 1,100 lines),
  and so is the engine's pager that only it used.
- Found on the way and fixed: in night mode the tajweed letters disappeared (the V4 fonts draw
  them in a black of their own); a night glyph filter now turns dark glyphs into the night ink and
  lightens the tajweed colors without changing their hue.
